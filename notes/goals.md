_Building the Bible reading, memorization, and language learning app of my dreams_

# SITE MAP

- three pagses:
    - memorize: ingest and retain bits of Scripture
    - read: see God's word in two languages at once
    - learn: build your language skills with God's word

## MEMORIZE

- Section dedicated to storing verses you want to memorize, are memorizing, and have memorized

- Each day, verses you are memorizing will be presented to you to read aloud once in full. Eventually, tap words on the verse to hide them next time, and you'll do your read aloud with progressively more of the verse hidden until you can do it all from memory. 
- Each day, one of your memorized verses will be presented to you. The chapter and verses will be presented, and you do your recite aloud. You have the option to edit the verse and make words you often forget visible, and the next day it will be presented to you. Memorized verses with un-hidden text will be put into the Rememorizing category; less frequent than memorizing but more than Memorized
- Each verse has a frequency timer that you can affect to help you. 
- perhaps john 3:16 is added as a default so that Learning > memorized verse retention mode always works

## READ

- Parallel reading
- Read-aloud in both languages
- Tap on English word to map to its Japanese equivalent. Shows 2 other examples where that Japanese word shows up in the Bible
- Either language can be turned on or off

## LEARN

- section dedicated to learning japanese language. there are different modes to help learn, kind of like Duolingo speak/listen/words
- verse of the day mode 
    - a verse (different from the one on the front page) is presented in english and you try to translate it. The location (Book X:X) is shown in English to help build associations
- memorized verse retention mode
    - at random, one of your memorized verses is presented to you blank. you must fill it in twice: once in english, and another time in japanese
    - text box underneath, you can type in any word. it'll fill correct words out in either language as you input them
    - buttons: show all, english hint (show next work in english), japanese (ditto for japanese)
- random mode
 - operates like VOTD mode, but it's any verse from anywhere in the bible
