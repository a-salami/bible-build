Building the Bible reading, memorization, and language learning app of my dreams

##MEMORIZATION

- Section dedicated to storing verses you want to memorize, are memorizing, and have memorized

- Each day, verses you are memorizing will be presented to you to read aloud once in full. Eventually, tap words on the verse to hide them next time, and you'll do your read aloud with progressively more of the verse hidden until you can do it all from memory. 

- Each day, one of your memorized verses will be presented to you. The chapter and verses will be presented, and you do your recite aloud. You have the option to edit the verse and make words you often forget visible, and the next day it will be presented to you. Memorized verses with un-hidden text will be put into the Rememorizing category; less frequent than memorizing but more than Memorized

- Each verse has a frequency timer that you can affect to help you. 

##READING & LANGUAGE LEARNING

- Parallel reading

- Read-aloud in both languages

- Tap on English word to map to its Japanese equivalent. Shows 2 other examples where that Japanese word shows up in the Bible

- Either language can be turned on or off

MISC

- Streak like YouVersion. As long as you’ve entered the app, you'll see and read the verse on screen.

- The ability to appear on top of apps: that image of Jesus looking around a corner at you should appear on your screen if you haven't read by 9 pm

- Bible verse picture saving like YouVersion, but it pastes both language's text in the picture

##TECHNICAL BUILD

- available on desktop (via website and launchable from taskbar) and on mobile (as a mobile-designed website to start; as an app as the end-goal)
